Processing Rules

For optimal classification accuracy, review our standard validation criteria:

Adequate Contrast

Avoid dim, dark backgrounds. The feature extraction layer relies on solid structural contours.

Clean Samples

For synthetic waste classes (plastics, glass, tin), please wash or empty them before categorizing.

EcoSort AI Platform

Empowering Municipalities with Machine Learning

EcoSort AI was designed to solve the sorting issues faced by municipal recycling plants and private waste facilities. Every year, millions of tons of recyclable glass, plastic, and metal are buried in landfill basins because materials are polluted or missegregated by consumers.

Our neural classifiers provide immediate clarity on material recyclability, pollution risks, and proper disposal sequences, lowering processing overhead and increasing global sorting efficiency.

Model Backbone MobileNetV3 (Weights pre-trained on ImageNet)
Classifier Head 6-Class Dense Softmax (Plastic, Paper, Glass, Metal, Organic, E-Waste)
Training Resolution 224 x 224 x 3 Channel Tensor Inputs
Recommendation Logic Hybrid Rule Engine + Material Density Metrics
Technical Validation

Disposal vs Classification

EcoSort AI clearly demarcates **Machine Learning prediction** (estimating physical material confidence score via CNN) from the **Rule-Based recommendation engine** (matching estimated classes with local recycling criteria). This prevents model hallucination in disposal procedures.
